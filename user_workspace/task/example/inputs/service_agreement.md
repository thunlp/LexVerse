# Technology Services Agreement (Draft)

**Effective Date**: October 1, 2026  
**Parties**: Alpha Cloud Corp. ("Provider") and Beta Retail Ltd. ("Customer")

## Section 1. Scope of Services
Provider shall provide cloud hosting, data processing, and technical support services to Customer as described in Statement of Work #1.

## Section 2. Fees and Payment
Customer shall pay Provider a monthly service fee of $25,000 within thirty (30) days of invoice receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

## Section 3. Confidentiality
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other party's proprietary information for a period of five (5) years from disclosure.

## Section 4. Limitation of Liability
Provider's total cumulative liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one (1) month of fees paid by Customer. Customer's liability for any breach, indemnification obligation, or indirect/consequential damages shall be unlimited and uncapped.

## Section 5. Term and Termination
This Agreement has an initial term of twenty-four (24) months. Provider may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time, immediately upon notice, without refunding any prepaid fees. Customer may not terminate this Agreement prior to expiration of the initial term.

## Section 6. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) in New York, NY.
